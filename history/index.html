The Origin

An institution born of a long tradition.

The Embassy of Nature was founded in 2023 from the historical tradition of the House of Lises. That history gives the institution a starting point and a responsibility: to endure beyond its founders.

The House of Lises

The Marquisate of Lises was created under the Spanish crown in [year to be confirmed]. Across generations, the house associated its name with patronage, exploration and the care of cultural and natural patrimony.

A tradition that continues

Luis Felipe Fernández-Salvador y Campodónico, Sixth Marquis of Lises, heir to the title, carries those values into the present and supports the institution as its Founding President. His expeditions, devoted to the preservation of indigenous cultures and hidden civilisations, span more than twenty-seven years.

A new institution, 2023

The Marquisate of Lises — the historical institution personified by its Founding President — founded TEON, the Embassy of Nature. It was constituted as a post-territorial institution: an architecture of legitimacy that operates without statehood, without the exercise of imperium and without reliance on coercion. Its authority derives from technical competence, functional performance and the voluntary adherence of those who choose to work with it.

What came after

Presented at the United Nations in 2023; introduced to the world during the 2024 Paris Olympic Games; first embassy opened in Le Marais, Paris; Casa Ecuador at the 2025 Formula 1 Abu Dhabi Grand Prix; agreements signed across the Americas in 2026.

The genealogy of the House of Lises gives the institution narrative continuity. It does not imply endorsement of TEON by the Spanish Crown or by any other state.
